Raoul and Vicki founded Montsweag Brook Corporation in 1998 to design and build uniqu, high performance structures throughout New England. In 2007, we launched BungalowInBox to formalize our approach to efficient, beautiful construction. Today, we focus on careful collaboration with our clients to design/build enhancing structures.

Having built authentic timber frame homes for over 40 years, Raoul brings classic timber framing skills and extensive building experience to house-design and construction. With a degree in Physics from Harvard, he enjoys the art and science of construction almost as much as collaborating with clients. His buildings often surprise and delight our clients with unusual details of substance and proportion.

After working in marketing for over twenty years and spending family vacations raising timber frames and panelized structures, Vicki joined Raoul full-time in 2016. She supports their endeavor with her marketing and accounting expertise and provides onsite construction support and organization.

As a family business, Raoul & Vicki are fortunate to have their children, Oscar, a Mechanical Engineer, and Iris, an Applied Math major, join on site when they are available. Both grew up immersed in the building world and working onsite from a very young age.

Our team of Fassi cranes not only deliver our buildings to your site, they also allow us to build with a small, nimble crew. These provide the muscle to help us get the job done quickly and safely.

We love raising one of our super-efficient, pre-designed, pre-fabricated kits with owners present and engaged in the process. Each bungalow provides opportunity for celebration. Perhaps our favorite challenge is to adapt this method to a custom application, design, or architectural style.
