The Sturbridge: A 24' x 28' Timber Frame Home with Full Second Story

The Sturbridge is a timber frame home that offers a surprisingly expansive living area within an efficient footprint. Featuring a full second story and additional attic space, this two-bedroom, two-bath residence utilizes ridge-pole timber frame bent construction to maximize interior volume. The design is a complete architectural solution, featuring three distinct timber-framed entries—an entry-way porch, a south-side awning, and a covered East entry—that provide both beauty and protection from the elements. Wrapped in a high-performance SIP envelope, the Sturbridge is a bright, sustainable, and spacious home ideal for year-round living.
