Custom 20'x24' Timber Frame Pergola: Handcrafted Maine Hemlock

When our clients asked for a structure that could withstand heavy winter snow and intense summer sun, we designed this 20'x24' heavy-timber pergola with Maine-sourced Hemlock and finished it with Heritage oil. By integrating custom-fabricated metal pieces to protect horizontal surfaces from moisture and UV damage, we’ve ensured this outdoor living space will remain a focal point for decades

Optional Configurations:

8'x8', 10'x10', 12'x12', 14'x14', 16'x16', as well as rectangular footprints.

Offered in a variety of sizes and configurations with choices in wood species and exterior treatments.
