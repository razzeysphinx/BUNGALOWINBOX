Small Timber Frame Guest Cottage | 20'x20' ADU Kit

This timber frame 20 x 20 structure was built in Down East Maine as a guest cottage. By incorporating a day-light basement, our clients added extra living space to this building as well as a place for utilities and storage. The SIP panels provide insulation from the cold Maine winters and keeps the building cool during the summer.

This structure is big enough to have a standard, code-compliant staircase and provide a bedroom on the second floor.
