The Phippsburg: A 20' x 36' Off-Grid Timber Frame Cottage

The Phippsburg Cottage is a 20' x 36' handcrafted timber frame home designed for the unique demands of off-grid living on the Maine coast. This spacious 1.5-story residence features a first-floor master bedroom and an open dining area, with the option of two additional bedrooms on the second level. Engineered for maximum thermal efficiency, the structure is enclosed in a high-performance SIP envelope that makes it ideal for remote sites with limited utility access. To bridge the gap between indoor and outdoor living, we integrated a 10' x 24' handcrafted timber frame porch, offering a shaded sanctuary to enjoy the surrounding nature preserve.
