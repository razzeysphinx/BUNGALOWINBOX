16'x24' Madison Loft Bungalow

This structure is big enough to have a standard, code-compliant staircase and provide a bedroom on the second floor. With spacious floor layout, taller wall panels, and heavy timber beams, The Madison offers more loft headroom and cathedral ceiling to the peak. This building was assembled in 7 days on site including siding, window and door installation, and full metal roofing. The PANEL FRAME wall system is left open for clients to install wiring and utilities prior to insulating and installing interior wall surfaces. The SIP roof over timber rafters is complete with sheetrock ceiling ready for taping and mudding.
