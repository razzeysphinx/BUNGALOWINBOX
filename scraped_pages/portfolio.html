Custom Timber Frame Homes & Kits

At Bungalow in a Box, we believe your home should be as enduring as it is beautiful. Based in Woolwich, Maine, we specialize in energy-efficient timber frame structures designed for modern living. Whether you are dreaming of a custom barn house, a coastal guest cottage, or a high-performance ADU, our unique building system combines traditional mortise and tenon joinery with the advanced insulation of Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs).

Our portfolio represents a diverse range of "volumes"—from expansive event barns to intimate lakeside retreats—each engineered for lasting value and site-specific beauty. Explore our projects below, categorized by gable-end width, and imagine the possibilities for your own custom build.
