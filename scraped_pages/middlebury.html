Single-Story Timber Frame Cottage | 24' x 34' Middlebury

This structure offers single story living with one bedroom, 1 1/2 baths and an expansive living space. The building has a 16' front wall which captures incredible mountain views. The back wall is 9' and provides views of the woods behind the cottage. The structure is enclosed with SIP panels and a metal roof.
