The 10'x10' Timber Frame Gazebo & Custom Grill House

Built as a Grill House for our neighbor, this special ornamental structure will serve a variety of outdoor activities. Easily adapted with partial or full enclosure to provide additional protection from sun, wind and rain on exposed sites. Polycarbonate roof panels offer sunscreen and rain protection with durable, long-lasting finish. We have used this industrial quality roof on several projects over the years with outstanding satisfaction. Generous proportions, gracious curves, refined timber frame joinery offer an elegant, substantial structure in celebration of the great outdoors.

Offered in a variety of sizes and configurations with choices in wood species and exterior treatments.

Also consider 8'x8', 10'x10', 12'x12', 14'x14', 16'x16', as well as rectangular footprints.
