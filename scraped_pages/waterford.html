The Waterford: 14'x20' Prefab Guest Cottage Kit with Kitchen & Bath

The Waterford is an original BungalowInABox PANEL-FRAME structure. This lightweight, elegant building offers a comfortable layout with sleeping loft, living area, kitchen and a compact 3/4 Bath. The 4"x10" cross-beams are planed, sanded, and oiled to give a solid post and beam interior ceiling. The 6'x20' exterior porch affords a transition from indoor to outdoor activities.
